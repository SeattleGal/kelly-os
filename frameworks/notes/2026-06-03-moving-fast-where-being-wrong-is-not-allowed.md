# Moving Fast Where Being Wrong Is Not Allowed

Date: 2026-06-03

## Observation

A healthcare AI team delayed rollout after discovering retrieval vulnerabilities that could expose protected information.

The issue was not model quality.

The issue was that identity was not preserved throughout the retrieval process.

The team delayed launch, rebuilt the architecture, reran testing, and only shipped after risk was reduced.

## What Stood Out

The phrase:

"How do you move fast on AI in a place where being wrong is not allowed?"

## Potential Insight

Many AI discussions optimize for speed.

Some environments optimize for correctness.

Examples:

* Healthcare
* Medical devices
* Finance
* Security
* Compliance

In these environments, the cost of being wrong may exceed the value of being fast.

## Connection to Coordination Layer Hypothesis

The challenge was not generating intelligence.

The challenge was coordinating intelligence within constraints.

Examples:

* Security
* Compliance
* Identity
* Governance

## Open Question

As AI becomes more capable, do constraints become more valuable than intelligence?

Can organizations safely coordinate intelligence in environments where mistakes are unacceptable?
