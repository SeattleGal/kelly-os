# Productivity vs Coordination

Date: 2026-06-04

## Observation

Multiple sources report strong individual productivity gains from AI.

However, most organizations are not seeing transformational outcomes.

## Snowflake

Focus is shifting from AI projects to AI ecosystems.

Challenges include:

- evaluation
- reliability
- safety
- security
- memory
- context
- protocols

## Atlassian

Workers report productivity gains.

96% of organizations have not achieved transformation.

The highest-performing organizations focus on:

- shared knowledge
- AI-powered coordination
- human-AI collaboration

## Potential Insight

AI improves individual productivity first.

Organizational transformation occurs only when coordination improves.

Productivity scales individuals.

Coordination scales organizations.

## Open Question

Will the next decade of AI be less about intelligence and more about organizational coordination?
