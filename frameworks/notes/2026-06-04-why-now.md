# Why Now?

Metadata, identity, governance, and trust are not new problems.

Why are they suddenly becoming critical?

Possible answer:

AI dramatically lowers the cost of intelligence while increasing the scale and speed of action.

As action scales, coordination failures scale.

As coordination failures scale, metadata, identity, trust, and governance become increasingly important.

Open question:

What is the actual forcing function?

- Agents?
- MCP?
- Agent-to-agent communication?
- Autonomous execution?
- Something else?
