# Why Now?

Date: 2026-06-04

## Observation

Metadata, identity, governance, permissions, and trust are not new problems.

Why are they suddenly becoming important?

## Possible Answer

For years, organizations were asking:

"Can AI do the work?"

Increasingly, the answer appears to be yes.

The question is changing.

Organizations are now asking:

* Can we trust it?
* Can we measure it?
* Can we govern it?
* Can we afford it?
* Can we operate it at scale?

## Cloud Parallel

Cloud adoption did not stop because servers failed.

Organizations needed:

* IAM
* tagging
* networking
* observability
* FinOps

The technology worked.

The operating model was immature.

AI may be entering a similar phase.

## Working Hypothesis

The industry is shifting from AI experimentation to AI operations.

The bottleneck is moving from capability to coordination.
