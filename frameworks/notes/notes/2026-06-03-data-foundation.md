# Data Foundation

Date: 2026-06-03

## Observation

Every layer of the Coordination Layer Hypothesis depends on data.

Examples:

* Memory requires data.
* Metadata is data.
* Governance requires data.
* Security policies require data.
* Routing requires data.
* AI FinOps requires data.
* Observability requires data.

## Potential Insight

As AI systems become more capable, data quality becomes increasingly important.

Poor data does not simply produce poor outputs.

Poor data can produce poor coordination.

## Question

Does the AI era increase the value of data quality?

Historically:

Garbage in → garbage out.

Potentially:

Garbage in → coordinated garbage out.

## Working Thought

Intelligence may be abundant.

Coordination may become scarce.

But both depend on trustworthy data.

Without trustworthy data:

* intelligence is unreliable
* coordination is unreliable
* governance is unreliable

Everything else sits on top of that foundation.
