# OpenAI Codex Cloud

Date: 2026-06-03

## Observation

OpenAI is investing heavily in agent execution infrastructure.

Examples:

- Long-running tasks
- Parallel agents
- Isolated environments
- Reusable skills
- Human review

## Initial Reaction

This feels less like a coding assistant and more like management infrastructure for AI workers.

The interesting part is not the model.

The interesting part is the orchestration layer.

## Potential Implications

Agents may require:

- Identity
- Permissions
- Memory
- Skills
- Governance
- Observability

## Connection to Coordination Layer Hypothesis

Possible supporting evidence.

The industry appears to be investing in coordination and management infrastructure around intelligence rather than intelligence alone.
